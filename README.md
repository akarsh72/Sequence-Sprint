# 🎮 Sequence Sprint: A Fast-Paced Number Challenge 🎮

🚀 **Enhance your focus and speed** by identifying numbers in sequential order within a **10-second time frame**!  
⏳ *It may take a few seconds to load.* **[ [Play The Game Now](https://akarsh72.github.io/Sequence-Sprint/build/web) ]**

---

## 🎯 **Goal**
Click numbers from **1 to 25** in ascending order as quickly as possible. Test your memory, focus, and speed to achieve the **highest score**!

---

## 🕹️ **How to Play**
1. Click **'PLAY'** to start.
2. Memorize the numbers displayed in a 5x5 grid for **10 seconds**.
3. Click the numbers in **ascending order (1 to 25)** after they disappear.
4. Your score is the count of **sequential correct clicks**.
5. Click **'Restart'** to try again if you make a mistake.

---

## 🖱️ **Controls**
- Use your **mouse** to click numbers and buttons.
- **'PLAY'**: Starts the game.
- **'Restart'**: Restarts after a game over.
