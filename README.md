## 🚀 About the Game  
Test your memory, speed, and focus in this fast-paced number challenge!  
Can you identify numbers in the correct sequence within just **10 seconds**?  

💡 **Think fast. Click faster. Beat the clock!**  

---

## 🎯 How to Play  
1️⃣ Click the **"Play"** button to start.  
2️⃣ Numbers will briefly appear in a random grid.  
3️⃣ Click the numbers in **ascending order** as quickly as possible.  
4️⃣ Your score increases with every correct click.  
5️⃣ The game ends if time runs out!  

⏱️ **Tip:** Stay focused and act fast to achieve the highest score!  

---

## 🕹️ Controls  
✅ **Mouse:** Click on the correct numbers in sequence.  
✅ **Play Again:** Restart the game after a round ends.
