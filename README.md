# 🎮 Sequence Sprint: A Fast-Paced Number Challenge 🎮

🚀 **Enhance your focus and speed** by identifying numbers in sequential order within a **10-second time frame**!  
⏳ *It may take a few seconds to load.* **[ [Play The Game Now](https://akarsh72.github.io/Sequence-Sprint/build/web) ]**

---

## 🎯 **Goal**
- Click numbers from **1 to 25** in ascending order as quickly as possible.
- Test your memory, focus, and speed to achieve the **highest score**!

---

## 🕹️ **How to Play**
1. **Start the Game**: Click the **'PLAY'** button to begin.
2. **Memorize the Numbers**: A 5x5 grid will display numbers from **1 to 25** in random order for **10 seconds**.
3. **Click in Sequence**: After the numbers disappear, click them in **ascending order (1 to 25)**.
4. **Score**: Your score is the count of **sequential correct clicks**.
5. **Game Over**: If you click the wrong number, the game ends. Click **'Restart'** to try again!

---

## 🖱️ **Controls**
- **Mouse**: Use your mouse to click the numbers and buttons.
- **'PLAY' Button**: Starts the game.
- **'Restart' Button**: Restarts the game after a game over.

## 🛠️ **Features**
- **Dynamic Timer**: Only **10 seconds** to memorize the numbers!
- **Interactive Grid**: Click numbers in the correct sequence to score.
- **Score Tracking**: Compete with yourself to beat your high score.
- **Game Over Screen**: Displays your final score and offers a **'Restart'** option.

---

## 🚀 **Why Play Sequence Sprint?**
- **Improve Focus**: Train your brain to quickly identify and remember sequences.
- **Boost Speed**: Challenge yourself to complete the sequence faster each time.
- **Fun and Addictive**: A simple yet engaging game for all ages.

---

## 📝 **Tips for Success**
1. **Stay Calm**: Don’t rush! Take a moment to memorize the numbers before clicking.
2. **Practice**: The more you play, the better you’ll get at recognizing sequences quickly.
3. **Challenge Yourself**: Aim for a perfect score of **25**!
